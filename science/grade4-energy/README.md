# Pippy's Energy Quest – Grade 4 Science: Energy Conversions

Open `index.html` in a browser (no server needed).

- 62 topics from the class study guide and vocabulary list, in 6 sections: what energy is & its forms, light, heat & sound, electricity & circuits, energy sources & devices, science & engineering words.
- 15 multiple-choice questions per topic (930 total), 5 of each kind:
  - **Know it**: definitions and key facts.
  - **Examples**: pick the example / non-example (transparent vs. opaque, conductor vs. insulator...).
  - **Apply it**: short real-life situations (what energy goes in and comes out of a toaster, which way heat flows, where sound travels fastest...).
- A round asks one question per topic: "Quick 20" (20 random topics) or "Every topic". Filter by section and by question kind. Unused questions come first; answer order is shuffled.
- Results show the score per question kind and each missed topic with its study-guide fact, plus "Practice missed topics".
- "Practice the must-write sentences": type the sentences marked "Have to write" on the guide; missing or misspelled words are highlighted.

Edit `data/units.json` or `data/q*.json`, then run `python3 build.py` to rebuild `index.html` (it also validates the data).
