# Latitude & Longitude Quest

A single-page game for practicing the **Hemispheres** and **Latitude and Longitude** study guide.
Open `index.html` in any browser. No install needed, and it works on a tablet or phone too.

## Modes
- **Explore** – hover over a world map to read live coordinates, plus key vocabulary cards.
- **Pin Hunt** – click the map at a coordinate or on a capital city. Closer = more stars.
- **Mystery Place** – which continent, ocean or capital is at these coordinates? The six practice questions from the study guide are always included.
- **Hemisphere Blitz** – Northern or Southern? Eastern or Western?
- **Mini Test** – 15 random questions from the study guide (the map is available, like on the real test).

Stars and best scores are saved in the browser (localStorage).
